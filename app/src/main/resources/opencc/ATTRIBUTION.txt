STCharacters.txt, STPhrases.txt, TSCharacters.txt and TSPhrases.txt are unmodified
UTF-8 copies of the upstream data/dictionary files. MediaAnvil uses phrase-first
longest matching with character fallbacks for offline lyric script conversion.
This is a limited in-app converter, not the full OpenCC runtime or regional
vocabulary conversion pipeline. No lyrics are uploaded for conversion.
